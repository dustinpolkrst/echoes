Echoes is a side-scrolling puzzle platformer made in [LÖVE](https://love2d.org). You play a small amber lantern-spirit in a dark ruin. Press the echo key and a cyan ghost replays your last three seconds. The ghost is solid on top, so you can stand on it, ride it, or leave it on a pressure plate to hold a door open.

I'm Dustin. All of it so far was built in one long session on 2026-10-17. I directed the work and played each build. Claude Code wrote the code, the tests and the tools. It ran as a lead agent that planned, handed work to subagents, reviewed what came back and checked it in the running game. Claude drafted these posts and I edited them. They follow that session in order, mistakes included. Start with [what the game is]({{< relref "about" >}}), or read from the top.
