Echoes is a side-scrolling puzzle platformer made in [LÖVE](https://love2d.org). You play a small amber lantern-spirit in a dark ruin. Press the echo key and a cyan ghost replays your last three seconds. The ghost is solid on top, so you can stand on it, ride it, or leave it on a pressure plate to hold a door open.

These posts follow the game from its first build on, in order, mistakes included. Start with [what the game is]({{< relref "about" >}}), or read from the top.
