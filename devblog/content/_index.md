Echoes is a side-scrolling puzzle platformer made in [LÖVE](https://love2d.org). You play a small amber lantern-spirit in a dark ruin. Press the echo key and a cyan ghost replays your last three seconds. The ghost is solid on top, so you can stand on it, ride it, or leave it on a pressure plate to hold a door open.

The whole game so far was built in one long session on 2026-10-17 by Dustin, the director, working with Claude Code. A lead agent planned the work, handed code to subagents, reviewed it, and checked it in the running game. These posts follow that session milestone by milestone, including the parts that went wrong.
